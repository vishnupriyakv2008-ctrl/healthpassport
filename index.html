Under the hood

Three parts, held together by a trust layer.

Patient wallet

  • Encrypted vault
  • Personal keys (DID)
  • Consent controls

Schema translator

  • Reads PDF (OCR + AI)
  • Maps to FHIR / HL7
  • Translates terms

Hospital in Country B

  • Scans the QR
  • Gets structured data
  • Records fit their system

Trust layer

Decentralized ID proves who you are. Every share is consent-based, time-limited and logged.

Privacy by design

Your records stay yours, from phone to ER.

🔒 On-device encryption

Records are locked on your phone. No central database to breach.

🔑 Keys only you hold

A decentralized ID proves it is you, with no company in the middle.

⏱️ Consent with an expiry

Each QR share is time-limited and covers only what you pick.

📋 Audit trail

Every access is logged, so you always know who saw what.

🚑

Emergency mode

Patient unconscious? A pre-approved summary opens: blood group, allergies and medication only. Never the full history.

Hard problems, real answers

The risks we expect, and how we handle them.

A wrong translation could harm a patient
The patient confirms each field, and the original PDF always sits beside it.
Privacy laws differ: GDPR, HIPAA, India's DPDP
Consent-first design. Data stays with the patient and nothing central is stored.
Hospitals will not install new software
It works from a QR and a browser viewer, and exports FHIR to existing systems.
